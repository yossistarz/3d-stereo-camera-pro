_Last updated: 27 October 2026_

3D Stereo Camera Pro ("the app") is made by Yossi Starz, an individual developer in Israel ("we"). This policy explains
what happens to your information when you use the app. Short version: **your photos and videos stay on your device.
We don't run servers, the app has no accounts, ads or analytics of its own, and we never receive your photos.**

## What the app uses on your device
| Permission | Why | Leaves the device? |
|---|---|---|
| Camera | Taking 3D photos and videos | No |
| Microphone | Sound for videos | No |
| Photos and videos (media access) | Showing the photos and videos the app made in its gallery | No |
| Location (optional, off unless you turn on *Save location*) | Writing where a photo or video was taken into the file (EXIF / MP4 location) | Only if you allow the map, see *Map in photo details* |
| Internet | The small map in a photo's details, and Google Play purchases | See below |

Photos, videos, settings, filters you import and the results of the *Device check* are stored on your device. Android
may include the app's settings in your own device backup (Google account backup); you control that in Android's
settings.

## Data handled by services we use
- **Map in photo details (Google Maps SDK for Android) — optional.** If you allow maps, opening the details of a
  photo that contains a saved location asks Google Maps for a small map of that place. This sends the photo's
  coordinates to Google, together with technical data the Maps SDK collects itself: device and app information, crash
  reports, your IP address and a pseudonymous Maps SDK identifier. We use it only to draw the map, and we don't
  receive any of it. Google's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy. The app asks before showing
  the first map; you can choose *Ask first*, *Always* or *Never* any time in Settings › Metadata › Map in photo
  details. Without maps nothing is sent, and *Open in Maps* still opens the place in your own maps app.
- **Purchases (Google Play).** The free trial, the Pro unlock and promo codes are handled by Google Play. Google
  processes the payment and tells the app whether your Google account owns Pro; we never see your payment details.
  Google Play's terms and privacy policy apply.

## Reports you choose to send us
The app can export a diagnostic report (a zip in Downloads › 3D Camera Reports). Nothing is sent automatically. If you
email a report to us, it contains the camera and device model, Android version, measurements, the app's log and,
only if you turned it on, check photos (with location removed). It contains no account names, phone numbers or
device identifiers. We use reports only to support more devices and fix bugs, keep them in one restricted place,
delete them within 12 months, and delete them sooner if you ask. We never sell them or use them for advertising.

## Children
The app is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.

## Your rights and contact
You can ask us what we hold about you (normally nothing, unless you sent us a report or an email), and ask us to
correct or delete it: **yossistarz@gmail.com**. We answer within 30 days.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we update this page and the date above. Significant changes are also mentioned in the app's
release notes.
